About

I'm Gerrit, an illustrator from Bergen with a degree in visual communication. I started out drawing logos for black metal bands. These days I draw things that are a lot more colourful, and only slightly less strange.

Everything is drawn by hand, digitally, in Clip Studio Paint. When a scene needs it, I build my own reference models in Blender.
