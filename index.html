MEANWHILE, IN THE STUDIO…

I'm a Bergen, Norway-based illustrator with a degree in visual communication. I used to do logos for black metal bands and stuff. These days, I like to create illustrations that are a bit more whimsical and colorful and a bit less uh, well ... you know. I draw everything by hand digitally in Clip Studio Paint. And when I need to, I create my own references for a drawing in Blender.

Worked with — Dauthuz, Ghostheart Nebula, Vathr
